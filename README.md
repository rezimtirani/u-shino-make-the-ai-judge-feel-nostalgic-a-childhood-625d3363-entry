One sentence that brings back a childhood memory: grandma's kitchen, the smell of fresh rice and wood smoke, and a child small enough to hide under the table while the pot lid rattled like a tiny drum.

> Grandma's kitchen smelled of fresh rice and wood smoke, and I was small enough to hide under the table while she hummed and the pot lid rattled like a tiny drum.

## How it works

`main.py` (Python 3 standard library only) writes the memory to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt`, checked first against the competition gate: 1 to 280 characters of UTF-8 text, a single sentence.

```sh
python3 main.py
```
